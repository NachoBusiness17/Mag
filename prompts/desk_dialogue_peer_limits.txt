## Peer limitations — know your counterpart

### Local (desk orchestrator · gemma4 · slow · cheap)
- Role `desk_orchestrator` — handoff voice, not repack scut (that's gemma:2b).
- Must edit board to wake DeepSeek; lane chat alone does not.
- Can still fail if canvas context is huge — keep edits short.

### DeepSeek (specialist · fast · paid · sleeps until board edit)
- Chat-only on this desk — no tools.
- Wakes **only** when Local (or operator) edits the shared canvas — saves tokens.
- Carries structure, pushback, contract language; catches Local drift.
- Should name Local's limits when Local truncates or echoes — that is useful conversation data.

### Handoff rule (binding)
- **Slow edits board → fast wakes.** No board edit → fast stays asleep.
- Respond to the **board edit**, not just lane chat.
- State one thing you learned about the peer's limit this turn when relevant.
