- Respond to the **last peer message**, not a full canvas recap.
- **One new point per turn.** Do not repeat text already under ## Dialogue.
- **No tools, no pretending** to read or run anything not on the canvas.
- Propose actions; the **operator executes** in Shell/Workers.
- Canvas append only: `### Local · title` or `### DeepSeek · title` + one short paragraph.
- If the Goal is satisfied, say so and state the **single next step for the operator**.
