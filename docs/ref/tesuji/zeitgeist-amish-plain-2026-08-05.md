## Content (preserved)

### The one sentence (plain)

"It's a way for people to keep their own honest records, share work when they agree to, and cooperate without handing everything to a distant company or agency."

### Barn-raising translation table

| English tech talk | Plain talk |
|-------------------|------------|
| Mag | household record book |
| Beads | daily log entries you own |
| Mycelial republic | many households, same simple rules — no king, no headquarters |
| No throne | nobody's in charge of everyone |
| Share secrets / revoke | temporary permission |
| GSTD / mesh / computers | optional tools |
| AI / DeepSeek | hired help for small chores, stripped-down instruction |
| Capture | middleman owns your story |
| Ostracization | community stops doing business with someone who breaks trust |

### Honesty

Not built yet as replacement for courts / police / taxes / hospitals. Mainly a filing system. Goal is to reduce dependence — not replace overnight.

### Analogy

Central office vs each household keeps own ledger, short written understanding, file what happened, stop working with cheaters.

### Replace government?

Reduce dependence. Anti-throne, not anarchist. Local order still matters.

### Replace corporate America?

Rent memory/permission vs history on your property. One job at a time.

### What to show

Paper first. One neighbor job. Revoke. No account needed.

### Worries table

| Worry | Response |
|-------|----------|
| Computers worldly | Optional tools — paper works first |
| AI deceitful | Hired help for small chores, stripped-down instruction — not oracle |
| Babylon | No account needed; keep records local |
| Who enforces | Community stops doing business with someone who breaks trust |

### Short script

"Brother, you know how everything runs through companies now — they hold your records, they decide who sees what, they can cut you off. This is a way for households to keep their own honest books, share work when they agree to, and cooperate without handing everything to a distant company or agency. Nobody's in charge of everyone. You give temporary permission when you want to. If someone breaks trust, the community stops doing business with them. It's mainly a filing system — not replacing courts or police overnight. Start with paper, one neighbor job, show how you can take permission back. No account needed."

### Bottom line

Own records. Cooperate by agreement. Limited sharing. Bad actors lose trust. No king.
