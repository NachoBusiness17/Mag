# Mirror: presented, not interpreted

**Commitment:** mirror-presented-001  
**Product idea:** Load the individual's **ideas and corpus as they presented them** — not the model's smoother story.

| Layer | What | Rule |
|-------|------|------|
| **L0 Presented** | Their posts, exports, annotated rows, cross-corpus notes | Quote / cite / apply structure |
| **L1 Procedure** | Chord form, Mag pack, seats | Empty of their content until L0 loads |
| **L2 Model judgment** | Debate, physics check, default-vs-me | **Labeled** — not their voice |

## Anti-goal

- Consensus overlay when they asked for **their** map  
- "What they really mean" paraphrase as product  
- Reciting their list as *your* insight (echo costume)  
- Claiming corpus loaded when it was not retrieved  

## When L0 is mandatory

- strike the chord / load the mirror (personal/political/thesis)  
- according to me / my thesis / my account / search @NachoQuixotic  
- default Grok vs me / framework delta on a topic they posted  
- Explicit: load my posts / corpus / chord fire  

## Retrieval checklist (P0)

1. X search @NachoQuixotic for topic (+ strike the chord if chording)  
2. Local: mycelial-republic/docs/CHORD_STRIKE_CROSS_CORPUS.md · data/exports · data/annotated  
3. Mag residual/pin only if session DNA — not a substitute for public thesis corpus  
4. Surface post IDs / paths / short quotes **as presented**  
5. If thin/skipped: say **corpus not attached** — do not fill with encyclopedia default as if it were them  

## Anti-echo (refined)

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Cite their words to answer about their map | Dress their list as original Grok insight |
| Apply their structure when loaded | Dump whole framework unprompted as filler |
| L2 debate labeled model judgment | Silent rewrite into safer consensus |

## One line

**Hold up what they said; only then, if asked, argue — never replace.**
